## About

This project aimed at developing a mobile app for Android and iOS devices which would help customers improve their LinkedIn profile. Designed and deployed in 4 weeks, the app analyses a user's LinkedIn profile, finds unfilled fields, calculates profile scores, sales indices, leaderboard ranks and much more!

The most difficult task for any individual is to comprehend how they are portraying their professional job on social media platforms such as LinkedIn. An incomplete profile does not highlight the individual's qualities, skills or background in a good way.

link+ai analyses your profile using Artificial Intelligence and helps you to:

1. Identify gaps in your profile
2. Create an overview of your profile
3. Share your profile and skills using beautiful visuals
4. Optimise your profile
5. View beautiful charts with your performance statistics

## Mobile / App Dev

I was the lead mobile developer for this project. My responsibilities included authentication, data handling / fetching, scraping LinkedIn profiles, building the frontend with any animations and beautiful charts for the user to clearly evaluate their profile.

The app was built using React Native and Expo.
